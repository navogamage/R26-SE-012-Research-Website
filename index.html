01 · DOMAIN

Research foundation

Literature Survey

The TAF reviews image-based leaf disease and nutrient deficiency classification, hybrid deep learning for leaf analysis, and ensemble approaches for pest detection. It also examines hyperspectral imaging and electronic nose systems for adulteration detection and origin authentication. These studies demonstrate the potential of AI for black pepper health and quality assessment.

Research Gap

Existing studies largely address individual tasks and often rely on controlled datasets, offline processing or specialized laboratory equipment. The proposed system connects pest detection, leaf and berry disease analysis, severity scoring, export quality assessment and short-term price forecasting in one smartphone-based platform for practical field use.

Research Problem

Black pepper farmers and inspectors rely on slow, subjective manual checks to identify pests, diseases, nutrient deficiencies and quality issues. Missed early symptoms can reduce yields and export value. An accessible tool is needed to support timely crop diagnosis, consistent quality assessment and informed selling decisions using ordinary smartphone images.

Research Objectives

Develop an integrated AI-powered mobile system for early crop diagnosis and informed selling decisions. The four components aim to detect leaf diseases and nutrient deficiencies with severity scoring; identify pests and suggest treatments; grade berries and forecast export prices; and detect berry diseases with safe remediation advice that preserves export quality.

Methodology

Collect and annotate field images with agricultural expert guidance, alongside quality standards and historical market data. Preprocess and augment images, then train and evaluate YOLOv8 models for detection, classification and segmentation. Estimate severity, combine crop health outputs into quality scores, and develop price forecasts. Integrate and test the APIs, storage and trilingual mobile interface.

Technologies Used

YOLOv8 models support image detection, classification and segmentation, with PyTorch for deep learning and OpenCV for image preprocessing and feature extraction. Python and FastAPI provide backend inference APIs, Flutter delivers the English, Sinhala and Tamil mobile interface, and Firebase stores images and results. Time-series machine learning supports market price forecasting.
